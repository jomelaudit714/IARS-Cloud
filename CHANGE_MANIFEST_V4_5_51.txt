IARS V4.5.51 — LIVE SOURCE NOTIFICATIONS + OPEN & DELETE DEEP LINKS
Baseline: IARS V4.5.50 Auditor Current Month / Backlog Gantt

USER-REQUESTED CHANGES
1. Weekly Itinerary Approved / Returned-for-Revision notifications are created at the exact admin action and resolve the recipient through id/username/email/full-name aliases.
2. Admin Gantt monthly create/save/update creates a targeted notification for the assigned auditor.
3. Live notification polling remains isolated from the full app and is tightened from 3 seconds to 2 seconds.
4. Per-notification controls are simplified to one button: Open & Delete.
5. Open & Delete removes the notification only from the signed-in user's inbox; broadcast notifications remain available to other users.
6. Announcement/Information: opens a full-message dialog and deletes the notification from that user's inbox.
7. Weekly Itinerary: opens the Weekly Itinerary module and the exact itinerary record/image referenced by the notification.
8. Yearly Audit Gantt: opens Yearly Audit Gantt and the exact schedule row/month editor referenced by the notification.
9. Policies & Memoranda: opens the exact newly added document; the policy cache is refreshed only for this deep-link path.
10. Future notification sources use the generic action_page + source_type + source_id contract; navigation resolves matching future nav labels automatically.
