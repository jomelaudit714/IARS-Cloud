# IARS V4.4.97

## Policies & Memoranda table
- Long document titles now wrap naturally inside the Title cell.
- The entire row and its borders expand vertically according to the longest cell.
- Short metadata remains centered; long titles remain left-aligned for readability.
- Added stronger word wrapping for long words, circular numbers, hyphenated titles, and mixed punctuation.

## Successful form cleanup
- After a confirmed Policies & Memoranda upload, the file, folder selection, title, type, subject/process, custom subject, version, effective-date checkbox/date, and description reopen as clean inputs.
- The same success-only reset is applied to the shared document/workpaper upload form.
- Failed and duplicate uploads keep the entered values so they can be corrected without re-encoding.
